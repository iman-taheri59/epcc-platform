## Authority and lifecycle

Transcription is available to active project participants whose membership covers PIPING (or unrestricted discipline). They create classes for their own company and edit only their company's classes/rows. Other participating companies can read them. Archived projects are read-only. This is a transcription boundary, not new review/approval authority, and no new role grants were added.

Headers, notes and rows require an explicit version token and entry/change reason. A stale form is rejected rather than overwriting newer data. Header edits and numbered-note changes share the header version; each row has its own version. No delete endpoint is exposed. Source wording is user-entered and preserved; deliberate edits retain before/after evidence.

Project links are selected under the same project advisory lock used by the definition review workflow. SQL independently checks complete captured-policy APPROVE decisions and absence of a successor for a new link. It does **not** trust the declaration's legacy `review_state` column. Unreviewed, returned, in-review or superseded definitions cannot be newly linked. Already-pinned links stay visible after supersession, receive an advisory, and do not automatically switch to a successor; a later explicit reassignment is audited.

Reference-library presence is not independent verification of properties. Definition approval is not a guarantee that the PMS row matches it. Source discrepancies are advisory and do not prevent faithful transcription; structural invalidity, unavailable reference IDs, access violations and stale writes are refused.
